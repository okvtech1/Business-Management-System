1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how OKV Technology Consults ("OKV", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you or your organization use the OKV Business Management System ("the Service") — including the marketing site, the signup and login pages, the installable app, and the dashboards used by Admins and team members.

We built this Service to help small and growing businesses track sales, inventory, customers, suppliers, and team activity — online or offline. This policy is written to reflect exactly how that Service actually works, not generic boilerplate.

2. Who this policy applies to

  • Organization Admins — the person who signs up and creates an organization's account.
  • Team members — users an Admin creates within their organization (Cashiers, Managers, Store Officers, and similar roles).
  • Customers and suppliers — contacts your organization chooses to message through the Service (email, SMS, or WhatsApp). We process their contact details only on your organization's behalf and instruction.

3. Information we collect

Account information

  • Business/organization name, admin full name, email address (used as your login username), phone number, and a securely hashed password.
  • For team members: username, role assignments, and status, as set by the organization's Admin.

Business data you enter

  • Products, sales, expenses, purchases, and other records your team creates while using the Service.
  • Customer and supplier contact details your organization adds for its own record-keeping and messaging.
  • Internal team chat messages sent within your organization.

Payment-related information

  • Subscription plan, billing status, and payment history.
  • If you pay by card, payment is processed directly by our payment gateway partner (Paystack, Flutterwave, or Remita, depending on what's enabled) — we do not receive or store your card details.
  • If you pay by bank transfer, we collect the payment screenshot you upload and the business/contact details you submit, solely to confirm and reconcile your payment.

Device and usage information

  • Basic technical information (browser type, device type, connection status) needed to run the app and its offline mode reliably.
  • A local, on-device copy of your organization's records, stored in your browser's IndexedDB, so the app keeps working without an internet connection and syncs automatically once you're back online.

4. How we use your information

  • To create and operate your organization's account and dashboard.
  • To let your team record sales, manage inventory, and track business activity, online or offline.
  • To process and confirm subscription payments, and send billing/trial reminders.
  • To send messages you initiate to your own customers or suppliers (email, SMS, or WhatsApp), and internal messages between your own team members.
  • To respond to support requests and keep the Service secure and reliable.
  • To send you occasional service announcements (e.g. plan changes, maintenance notices). We do not send marketing email to your customers or suppliers on our own initiative — only what your organization directly composes and sends.

5. How your data is stored and isolated

Each organization's business records (products, sales, and other transactional data) are stored in a dedicated, private data store that is not shared with any other organization on the Service. Account, billing, and team-membership information is kept in a separate, tightly access-controlled system used to operate the platform. We do not mix one organization's business data with another's.

The Service's backend runs on Google's infrastructure (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script). This means your data is subject to Google's own security safeguards in addition to ours. We do not sell access to your data to any third party, and we do not use your business data to train any AI or machine-learning model.

6. Third parties we work with

We use a small number of service providers to operate the platform. Each only receives the information it needs to do its specific job:

  • Google (Sheets, Apps Script, Drive) — hosts the application backend and stores organizational data.
  • Payment gateways (Paystack, Flutterwave, Remita) — process card payments directly, where enabled. We only receive confirmation of a successful or failed payment.
  • SMS and WhatsApp messaging providers — deliver messages your organization chooses to send, where automatic sending is enabled for your plan.
  • Email delivery services — deliver account, billing, and support emails.

We do not permit these providers to use your data for their own purposes beyond delivering the service we've engaged them for.

7. Data retention

We keep your organization's data for as long as your account is active, plus a reasonable period afterward in case you wish to reactivate or export it. If you close your account and request deletion, we will delete your organization's data within a reasonable timeframe, except where we're required to retain certain records (e.g. payment records) to meet legal or accounting obligations.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or request deletion of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Organization Admins can already access, edit, and delete most of their organization's data directly from the dashboard. For anything else — including a full data export or full account deletion — contact us using the details below and we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Cookies and local storage

The Service does not use third-party advertising cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in, and IndexedDB to store an offline copy of your organization's records on your own device so the app keeps working without a connection. Clearing your browser's site data will sign you out and remove the local offline copy (your data remains safe on our servers once synced).

10. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the Service is not directed at them.

11. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your information, including password hashing, per-organization data isolation, and access controls limiting who can view or modify your organization's data. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to keep your data protected and will notify affected organizations promptly if we become aware of a breach affecting their data.

12. International data transfers

Because our infrastructure runs on Google's global cloud platform, your data may be processed on servers located outside Nigeria. We take reasonable steps to ensure any such transfer is handled with the same level of protection described in this policy.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the Service evolves. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do, and for significant changes, we'll make a reasonable effort to notify Organization Admins directly.
