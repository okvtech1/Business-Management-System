Privacy Policy Terms of Service

Short version: Every paid plan is covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee. If the Service isn't right for you, tell us within 30 days of a payment and we'll refund it — no lengthy justification required.

1. What's covered

The 30-day money-back guarantee applies to your first payment on a new paid subscription, and to each subsequent renewal payment, for as long as you remain a customer. Each individual payment gets its own 30-day window from the date it was made.

2. How to request a refund

Email technologyokv@gmail.com within 30 days of the payment you'd like refunded, with your business/organization name and the email address on your account. We may ask a quick optional question about why the Service didn't work out — this is only to help us improve, not a condition of the refund.

3. Processing time

  • We aim to confirm approved refund requests within 2 business days of receiving them.
  • Refunds for card payments are returned to the original payment method via the payment gateway used (Paystack, Flutterwave, or Remita) and typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.
  • Refunds for manual bank transfer payments are sent back to the bank account the payment came from, and typically complete within 5 business days of approval.

4. What happens to your account

Once a refund is processed, your subscription for that billing period ends and your organization's access reverts to trial/limited status. Your business data isn't deleted when you request a refund — it's handled the same way as any other account, per our Privacy Policy. If you decide to come back later, your historical records are still there.

5. Exceptions

  • Requests made more than 30 days after the relevant payment aren't covered by this guarantee, though you're welcome to contact us — we review these on a case-by-case basis.
  • This policy covers subscription payments for the Service itself. It doesn't cover third-party charges outside our control (e.g. your own mobile data costs, or your bank's transfer fees).
  • Repeated sign-up-refund cycles on the same organization or by the same person may be reviewed and declined at our discretion, to keep the guarantee fair for everyone.

6. Questions

If anything here is unclear, or your situation doesn't quite fit the cases above, just reach out — technologyokv@gmail.com or +234 810 414 1138. We'd rather have a quick conversation than leave you guessing.
